    The session clock

    Every day runs on New York time

    Times shown in ET, with your local time beside them.

    1. 12:00ET · –

      Session opens

      Yesterday's winner ships and the floor opens. Any holder above the line can table a motion.

    2. 08:00ET · –

      Motions close

      The Order Paper locks. No new coins join, so the last four hours are a straight race.

    3. 11:59ET · –

      Floor closes

      Every signed ballot is counted against a snapshot of $QUORUM balances, and turnout is checked against quorum.

    4. 12:00ET · –

      It ships

      The winning motion launches on pump.fun from the public ship wallet. The next session opens the same second.

    Rules of the house

    Simple enough to fit on a ballot

    Ballots
    One wallet, one ballot per session. Signing is free and moves no funds.
    Weight
    Your ballot counts the $QUORUM you hold at 11:59 ET.
    Quorum
    At least 5% of supply has to vote. No quorum, no coin.
    Ship wallet
    Launches the winner and never buys it. No dev buy, no bundle, every launch public on-chain.
    Tabling
    Hold 100K $QUORUM to table a motion.

    Where the fees go

    Every coin we ship pays $QUORUM.

    Each winner earns creator fees on every trade. Most of it buys $QUORUM back. The rest goes to the wallet that tabled the winning motion, so good ideas get paid.
